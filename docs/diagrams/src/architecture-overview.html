Watch
Notice what matters
Five detectors run continuously over every track: silences, shared identities, loitering, meetings at sea, closed-area entries. They are arithmetic, not opinion.

An agent reads each candidate and raises an alert with its evidence and time window, or dismisses it and says why.
Investigate
Build the case
Two lines of enquiry run at once: who is this vessel and who controls it, and what did it do. Each can only use approved tools over the record.

Code, not a model, merges the two, keeps every claim tied to its evidence, and names what is still unknown.
Propose
Write it up, suggest a next step
A Vessel of Interest report: timeline, indicators, counter-indicators, confidence, gaps. Every indicator cites its evidence or the report is rejected.

If imagery would settle the question, a collection request is proposed. Nothing is ordered.
